01

Drawings and take-off

Pipework, ductwork, containment, fittings and equipment are taken off on the drawing itself. A new revision is laid over the old one with every change boxed, and once the take-off is signed off, anything the drawings say differently is listed as a possible variation.

Progress

Recorded on the drawing: tap the runs that are done, stage by stage, with the photos behind them. The office approves what site records, and a stage that needs a test counts only once a passed test covers it.

03

Variations

One register from the instruction to the agreement, including work not instructed yet. Each step writes its own letter: the notice, the confirmation of a verbal instruction, the quote. And what the work took is set against what was agreed.
